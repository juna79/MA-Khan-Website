01 / Our story

Kenyan roots.
Lasting relationships.

M.A. Khan Insurance Brokers Ltd is a fully-fledged Insurance brokerage firm licensed by the Insurance Regulatory Authority of Kenya (IRA) to operate as a broker in Kenya.

The firm was incorporated in 1960 by the late Mohamed Afzal Khan as an Insurance Agency and due to its growth, it transitioned into an Insurance Brokerage firm in 1999. Our continued existence for more than six decades speaks volumes about our commitment to the Kenyan market and the entire East African Region.

Home/Claims

Support with your claim.

We help you understand the next steps and support you through the claims process.

Need support?

Email our teamAll contact details

We all dread it when the inevitable happens and we have to lodge a claim with the insurance company, it is inconvenient and a very stressful process. With the help of our team, we shall walk you through the crucial steps that will lead to processing your claim

Important note for all claims:
  • Report all claims to us as soon as possible
  • Obtain a police abstract
  • Quick submission of all the required documents/information
  • Ensure that you provide full details of the of loss/damage. If possible, take & produce photographs of the scene before it is disturbed to support the loss /damage
  • Ensure that you take measures to minimize the loss
  • Where a third party is involved NEVER ADMIT LIABILITY
Motor Claims

Own damage / liability

  • Note details of the location, date, and time of the accident and possible cause of the accident
  • In all cases of bodily injury, report to the Police immediately and note to get names and details of as many witnesses as possible and obtain an inspection report.
  • In cases involving property damage, take details of the third party (Name, contact details, Description of their vehicle, their Insurers)
  • If the vehicle is not drivable, arrange for the vehicle to be towed to an approved garage/repairer.
  • Please obtain a repair quotation from an approved Garage/Repairer (Comprehensive Policies Only). If you do not know of any, please contact us and we will provide you with a list of approved repairers by the Insurer. Don’t authorize repairs without the consent of the insurers.
  • Assessment will then be arranged at the Repairer’s premises
  • Share the duly completed Claim Form and return to us with clear and legible copies of your Driver’s License, copy of the log book, and Police Abstract.
  • Advise us of any Third Party Claims and forward to us any correspondence received from the Third Party, without acknowledging the same.

Windscreen claims

Complete a claim form and forward it to us with the following documents.

  • Original replacement receipt with ETR
  • Photo of the damaged windscreen before and after replacement
  • Copy of logbook.
  • Copy of driver’s valid driving license.
  • Replace the damaged windscreen
Fire & Perils /Fire Consequential Loss Claims
  • Report all claims to us as soon as possible
  • Submit all the required claim documentation at the earliest
  • If possible, establish the estimate of the loss/damage or provide a breakdown of the loss /damage
  • We will ensure that a Loss adjuster is appointed depending on the loss estimate
  • Ensure that you take measures to minimize the loss
  • Ensure that your Accounting Records are intact as without such records, claim settlement could be prejudiced
  • Don’t authorize repairs without the consent of the insurers
Machinery Breakdown Claims
  • Notify us immediately in case of accidental electrical or mechanical breakdown of a machine.
  • Obtain the technical report on the cause of damage and submit it together with repair/replacement estimates
  • Submit all the required claim documentation at the earliest
  • Partial Loss: The damaged part is replaced with no depreciation charged
  • Total Loss: Item is replaced
Property Loss or Damage Claims
  • Report all claims to us as soon as possible
  • Submit all the required claim documentation at the earliest
  • Obtain replacement quotations for all damaged or stolen items and attach copies of original purchase invoices or replacement invoices. Don’t authorize repairs without the consent of the insurers
  • If the claim involves stolen jewellery, then please include recent Valuation Certificates.
  • All crime-related losses must be reported to the nearest Police Station and a Police Abstract must be obtained.
  • If items are damaged by lightning, water, fire, etc. then a Damage Report must be included with the claim documentation stating that the items are damaged beyond economical repair and/or repairable and what the cause of damage was. If the items can be repaired, the repair cost must be given on the Damage Report.
  • All damaged items must be retained until the claim is settled as the Insurance Assessor may want to view the items or collect the salvage.
Marine Claims
  • Advise us of the loss as soon as possible.
  • If you discover any loss or damage whilst off-loading, and if possible, wait for a Loss Adjuster to be present, if not please take pictures showing loss or damage.
  • Whenever goods are received but contents are not checked e.g. cartons, mark the Inland Consignment note to read ‘Contents not verified or checked’.
  • Whenever you receive goods which may be damaged or there are obvious shortages, insist that a Transporters Representative be present whilst the goods are checked.

The following documents are normally required:

  • Marine Certificate
  • Original or Non-negotiable Bill of Lading.
  • Original Invoice(s) and Packing List.
  • Inland Transporters Consignment.
  • Port Documents E.g. Release Order, Port Charges, Port-Examination Voucher, if issued, etc.
  • Survey Report.
  • Correspondence exchanged with Third Parties.

For major losses, Insurers directly appoint Surveyors or Loss Adjusters who provide the following services:

  • Inspect and determine nature/extent of loss
  • Investigate and determine cause of loss
  • Assist in loss mitigation and salvage disposal
  • Assess quantum and negotiate claims settlement
  • Obtain evidence of loss e.g. photographs, expert reports and documents
  • Initiate recovery against liable carriers or bailees
  • Issue detailed Survey/Adjustment report to Insurers.
Fidelity Guarantee Claims
  • Notify us immediately if you discover a loss / have reason to believe there has been an act of fraud or dishonesty involving one or more of your employees.
  • Report to the police/authorities for further investigation to be carried out.
  • Withhold any salary/wages due to the fraudulent employees.
  • Submit all the required claim documentation at the earliest including, a duly completed claim form, Appointment letter, Reference letters, Police abstract, Termination letter, Copy of ID for the suspect and other relevant documentation to support the claim.
WIBA Claims
  • Notify the Director of Occupational Safety & Health Services within 24 hours in writing for any Fatal Injuries and within 7 days in case of other Injuries if the employee has been away from work for more than 72 hours
  • Advise us of the Injury as soon as possible.
  • Ensure that an Incident Report has been made and signed by the Supervisor and at least two witnesses when the incident is still fresh in their minds.
  • For all Companies with more than 20 employees, a Health and Safety Committee must be constituted. Whenever an employee is injured, the Committee must submit a Report.
  • Submit all the required claim documentation at the earliest

Claim Documentation

An Injury not leading to Permanent Total Disability:

  • Claim Form
  • DOSH I which acts as a claim form.
  • DOSH II which should be completed by the attending doctor, acts as the medical certificate
  • Original Medical Bills
  • Medical Receipts
  • Copy of certified pay slip for one month prior to the date of the incident
  • Copy of national identity card
  • Statement by the injured party/ employee
  • Statement by a witness.

Claim Documentation

An Injury leading to any degree of Permanent Total Disability:

  • This is processed six months after an injury.
  • The documents stated above will have already been submitted and medical expenses and Temporary Total Disability bit of the claim paid.
  • The injured should obtain a medical certificate and forward the same to both the DOSH office and to the insurers.
  • The DOSH office will compute the benefits payable on a form called DOSH WIBA 4 form and if the claimant is in agreement with the computations, then they sign three (3) copies of DOSH WIBA5/A. These forms are provided to the claimant at any DOSH office.

Claim Documentation

If the injury is fatal please provide the following additional documents

  • Death Certificate
  • Post-mortem Report
  • Death Permit
  • List of Beneficiaries

N.B DOSH form parts I & II- are obtained from the labour office. An incident report and copy of the claimant’s payslip need to be submitted to the labour office to get these documents done DOSH form part II will indicate the benefit payable for TTD.

Who is an Insurance Broker?

An insurance broker is a licensed professional and independent advisor knowledgeable in risk management and insurance services.

Who do insurance brokers work for?

An Insurance broker is an intermediary who typically works with multiple insurance companies offering a variety of products and services to a customer (individuals/businesses)

They research the market for suitable options, share those options with their clients, and help them apply for and purchase coverage from insurance companies.

Why should I use an insurance broker?

There are many reasons why using an insurance broker is the best way to protect you or your business, here are some of the reasons.

  • Cost- Brokers research the market for suitable options ensuring that the client not only gets value for money but also gets the right cover, benefits, and limits.
  • Expertise – Insurance policies can be complex and there are often many differences in coverage between policies from different insurers. An insurance broker simplifies the jargon used in Insurance policies, and advises and recommends the most ideal package for your circumstances.
  • Ease – Identifying the right cover, negotiating the best terms and most competitive premium as well as filing a claim is not an easy task. We ensure that the entire insurance process is a simple and hassle-free experience for our clients.
  • Speed – An insurance broker understands the claims process and will provide guidance and assistance in expediting the claims process.
  • Peace of Mind– We are there to offer advice, guidance, and advocate for you at all times to ensure that you can focus on your core activities.
What insurance cover does my business need?

Every business faces different risks and exposures, which is why we tailor Insurance specific to your business. We handle all areas of commercial insurance for our clients whose risk exposures are broad and multifaceted. Our clients hail from different sectors of the economy including financial services, SME, NGO, manufacturing, construction, technology, pharmaceuticals, storage, hospitality, transport & logistics, etc.

What type of personal insurance might I need?

Just like a business, you are likely to have assets, whether it is a Car, or your Home, not forgetting yourself. We offer Medical Insurance, Life Insurance, Pension, Travel insurance among other policies depending on your specific needs.

Who decides the Insurance premium rates?

Insurance Premium rates are based on the type of risk, however, there are tariffs in the market that guide on premium rating for several Insurance classes. In Kenya, premium rates are governed by;

  • Reinsurers- Insurers have to comply with rating structures required by their Reinsurers to avoid invalidating their Reinsurance contracts.
  • The Association of Kenya Insurers also advises premium rates and tariffs for certain classes of business.
  • The office of the Insurance Regulatory Authority also guides on rates /tariffs of some classes of business.
How much will the insurances cost me?

Almost every risk is different, and we will tailor the insurances required to suit you. We listen, evaluate your needs, and recommend/ advise our clients on the best solution. Depending on the type of risk and the sum insured, we will source and compare terms from various providers and recommend the best package for you.

How do I pay my Insurance premium?

Insurance Amendment Act No.11 2019 states that all insurance premiums relating to any insurance policy will be paid directly to the insurance company issuing such policy. However, where the insurance premium is payable by cheque, the cheque should be written in the name of the insurance company and such cheque can be delivered to us as your broker to dispatch to the Insurer.

Can I pay the Insurance premium in Installments?

The Insurance Act requires premiums to be paid upfront (Cash & Carry). However, at M.A. Khan Insurance Brokers, we can arrange for you to pay the premium in Installments through Insurance Premium Finance (IPF) from various financial institutions.

What happens to my policy if I fail to pay the premium?

Failure to pay Insurance premiums as stipulated in the policy amounts to a breach of policy terms and conditions and leads to the termination of the contract. This also means that you cannot make a claim.

What is an Excess/Deductible?

An excess is an amount the policy holder must pay if they proceed with making an insurance claim. This is usually indicated in your policy document as a fixed amount or as a percentage of the limit, sum insured or the loss.

Are there cases where the Excess is not payable?

Yes, this is mostly applicable to Motor Insurance Policies.

  • If you have purchased an Excess Protector Benefit, you will not be required to pay the excess amount as long as the loss/damage is above the Excess limit.
  • If your policy has a No blame no Excess benefit, you will not be required to pay any excess if you are not to blame for the accident. This is subject to provision of a police abstract noting the same.
